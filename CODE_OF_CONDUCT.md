# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Argue about the work, not the person.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels
- Give and accept constructive criticism gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users
- Show empathy towards other contributors
- Use welcoming and inclusive language

This is an international project shipping in ten languages. Many contributors
are not writing in their first language. Judge the substance of what someone
says, not their grammar.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Discriminatory language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Deliberately derailing discussions or exhausting other contributors
- Sustained disruption of discussions
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions — and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers privately through GitHub.
Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainers will review, respond, and
take action proportionate to the situation: a warning, a temporary ban, or a
permanent ban.

Maintainers who do not follow this code in good faith may face the same
consequences as anyone else.
